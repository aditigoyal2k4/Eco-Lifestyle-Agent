# Reducing Plastic Pollution at Home

## Single-Use Plastic Alternatives
- Replace plastic bags with reusable cotton, jute, or recycled nylon bags. A single reusable bag can replace 500-700 plastic bags over its lifetime.
- Switch from plastic water bottles to stainless steel or glass. An estimated 480 billion plastic bottles are sold globally each year.
- Use beeswax wraps or silicone food covers instead of cling film/plastic wrap.
- Choose bar soap, shampoo bars, and conditioner bars to eliminate plastic pump bottles.
- Use a bamboo toothbrush instead of a plastic one — over 4.7 billion plastic toothbrushes go to landfill each year.

## Kitchen Plastic Reduction
- Buy loose produce and store in reusable mesh bags — avoids unnecessary plastic packaging.
- Choose glass or cardboard containers over plastic wherever possible.
- Use a compostable bin liner or no liner at all for food waste.
- Invest in a reusable coffee cup — over 16 billion disposable coffee cups are used each year.
- Avoid microwave-in-bag and single-serve packaging.

## Bathroom & Personal Care
- Switch to a safety razor with replaceable metal blades instead of disposable plastic razors.
- Choose toothpaste in glass jars or toothpaste tablets in cardboard boxes.
- Use menstrual cups or reusable period pads as alternatives to single-use products.
- Buy in bulk to reduce per-unit packaging waste.

## Recycling Plastic Correctly
- Only plastics labelled #1 (PET) and #2 (HDPE) are accepted in most kerbside collections.
- Always rinse plastic containers before recycling — contaminated plastics go to landfill.
- Soft plastics (bread bags, cling wrap) must go to dedicated soft plastic collection points, not kerbside bins.
- Black plastic is often not detectable by sorting machines and goes to landfill — avoid it.

## Impact Statistics
- Humans produce 400 million tonnes of plastic waste every year (UNEP, 2023).
- Only 9% of all plastic ever produced has been recycled.
- Plastic takes 400–1000 years to decompose in a landfill.
- Replacing single-use plastics in your household can eliminate 3–5 kg of plastic waste per month.
