# Home Energy Saving Guide

## Lighting
- Switch all bulbs to LED — they use 75% less energy and last 25x longer than incandescent bulbs.
- Use smart plugs or timers to ensure lights turn off automatically.
- Make the most of natural daylight before switching lights on.
- Motion sensors in hallways, bathrooms, and outdoor areas prevent lights being left on.

## Heating and Cooling
- Every 1°C reduction in thermostat setting saves approximately 10% on heating bills.
- Install a smart or programmable thermostat — can cut heating energy by up to 23%.
- Draught-proof doors, windows, and letterboxes. Draughts account for up to 25% of heat loss.
- Cavity wall insulation can save up to 140 kg of CO₂ per year.
- Loft/attic insulation saves up to 250 kg of CO₂ per year.
- Use curtains and blinds to retain heat in winter and block heat in summer.
- Set your water heater to 60°C — higher wastes energy, lower risks legionella.

## Appliances
- Washing clothes at 30°C uses 40% less energy than washing at 60°C.
- A full dishwasher uses less water than hand-washing — run it on eco mode.
- Defrost your fridge-freezer regularly — ice buildup increases energy use by 30%.
- Turn off appliances at the wall — standby mode accounts for up to 10% of household electricity use.
- When buying new appliances, choose A-rated or A+++ energy efficiency class.
- An air fryer uses up to 75% less energy than a traditional oven for small meals.

## Renewable Energy at Home
- Rooftop solar panels can generate 3–4 kWh per day per kW of installed capacity.
- A typical home solar system (4kWp) can save 1–1.5 tonnes of CO₂ per year.
- Solar water heaters can provide 50–70% of a home's hot water for free from sunlight.
- Battery storage systems allow you to use solar energy at night.
- Switching to a 100% renewable energy tariff immediately reduces your carbon footprint.

## Electric Vehicles
- Electric vehicles (EVs) produce 70% fewer lifetime emissions than petrol cars.
- Charging an EV at home overnight on off-peak tariff costs roughly £2–3 per 100 miles.
- A 7kW home charger fully charges most EVs in 6–8 hours.

## Impact Statistics
- Households account for 20% of global energy consumption (IEA, 2023).
- LED lighting worldwide could save 1,400 TWh of electricity per year — equivalent to shutting 230 large power plants.
- Smart home devices can cut household energy consumption by 10–15%.

## Today's Eco-Action
Switch off all devices at the wall before bed tonight — not just on standby. This one habit alone can cut £30–50/year from your electricity bill.
