# Sustainable Transport Guide

## Carbon Footprint by Transport Mode (per passenger-km)
- Short-haul flight: 255 g CO₂e/km
- Long-haul flight: 195 g CO₂e/km
- Petrol car (single occupant): 170 g CO₂e/km
- Diesel car (single occupant): 155 g CO₂e/km
- Electric car (UK grid): 53 g CO₂e/km
- Bus: 89 g CO₂e/km (goes to ~18g when full)
- Coach: 27 g CO₂e/km
- Train (national rail UK): 41 g CO₂e/km
- Eurostar: 6 g CO₂e/km
- Cycling: 0 g CO₂e/km (manufacturing ~5 g CO₂e/km)
- Walking: 0 g CO₂e/km

## Active Travel (Walking & Cycling)
- 50% of all car journeys in the UK are under 5 miles — easily cyclable.
- Cycling to work 3 days per week saves 2.4 tonnes of CO₂ per year.
- E-bikes extend the viable cycling range to 15–30 km with minimal effort.
- Walking for short trips also improves cardiovascular health — 10,000 steps per day reduces heart disease risk by 30%.
- Cargo bikes can replace a car for family shopping trips and school runs.

## Public Transport
- Switching one car commute to rail or bus saves 2.2 kg CO₂ per day.
- Monthly bus/train passes are significantly cheaper than running a car.
- Cities with good public transport see 30–40% lower per-capita transport emissions.
- Apps like Citymapper, Google Maps, and Transit help plan multi-modal journeys.

## Electric Vehicles
- EVs have 70% lower lifetime emissions than equivalent petrol/diesel cars.
- Home charging overnight on renewable tariff brings emissions close to zero.
- Government grants (OZEV in UK, IRA in USA, FAME in India) reduce EV purchase cost.
- Used EVs are now available from £8,000–£15,000, making them increasingly accessible.

## Flight Reduction
- A single return flight from London to New York produces ~1.7 tonnes CO₂e — equivalent to 3 months of average UK household emissions.
- Take the train for journeys under 600 km — often quicker door-to-door and far lower carbon.
- If flying is unavoidable, choose direct economy class (no layovers, no business class — both higher impact).
- Offset unavoidable flights through verified schemes (Gold Standard, VCS).

## Car Sharing and Car Clubs
- Car club membership (e.g. Zipcar, Co-wheels) saves the average household £3,000/year vs. car ownership.
- Sharing a car journey with one other person halves the per-km emissions.
- Platforms like BlaBlaCar enable long-distance ride-sharing.

## Today's Eco-Action
For your next journey under 5 km, choose walking, cycling, or public transport instead of driving. Track the CO₂ saved using the figures above.
