# Sustainable Food and Diet Guide

## Plant-Based Eating
- Producing 1 kg of beef emits 27 kg of CO₂e; 1 kg of tofu emits only 2 kg CO₂e.
- Replacing one beef meal per week with a plant-based alternative saves 52 kg CO₂ per year.
- A fully plant-based diet can reduce food-related greenhouse gas emissions by up to 73%.
- You don't need to go fully vegan — even reducing meat to 3 meals per week makes a significant difference.
- Good protein alternatives: lentils, chickpeas, beans, tofu, tempeh, quinoa, oats, nuts and seeds.

## Seasonal and Local Food
- Seasonal produce requires no heated greenhouses and travels shorter distances — up to 50x less CO₂ than imported out-of-season equivalents.
- Visit farmers' markets or join a Community Supported Agriculture (CSA) box scheme for local seasonal produce.
- Grow your own herbs, salad greens, and tomatoes — even on a windowsill.
- Choose locally caught fish over imported farmed fish where possible.

## Reducing Food Waste
- One-third of all food produced globally is wasted — contributing 8% of global greenhouse gases (FAO).
- Plan meals for the week before shopping — reduces impulse buying and spoilage.
- Store food correctly: keep herbs in water, store bread in a bread bin, use the freezer proactively.
- Use the FIFO method (First In, First Out) — bring older items to the front of the fridge.
- Wilted vegetables are perfect for soups, stews, and stir-fries.
- Compost unavoidable food scraps rather than sending them to landfill.

## Sustainable Seafood
- Choose seafood certified by the Marine Stewardship Council (MSC) — blue label.
- Avoid species on the WWF red list (e.g. Atlantic cod, bluefin tuna, tropical shrimp).
- Eat lower in the food chain — sardines, mackerel, mussels, and oysters have a very low environmental footprint.
- Farmed bivalves (mussels, oysters, clams) actually improve water quality.

## Organic and Fair Trade
- Organic farming uses no synthetic pesticides and supports biodiversity.
- Fair trade certification ensures farmers in developing countries receive fair wages.
- Look for the Rainforest Alliance, UTZ, and Fairtrade marks on coffee, chocolate, and bananas.

## Impact Statistics
- The food system is responsible for 26% of global greenhouse gas emissions (Our World in Data, 2023).
- Food waste in the UK alone is worth £17 billion per year.
- If food waste were a country, it would be the third-largest emitter of greenhouse gases.

## Today's Eco-Action
Plan your meals for the next 3 days and make a shopping list — only buy what you need. This single habit can halve your household food waste.
