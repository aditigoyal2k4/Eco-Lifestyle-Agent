# Government Renewable Energy Schemes and Incentives

## India — Solar Energy Schemes

### PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana (2024)
- Provides 300 units of free solar electricity per month to 10 million households.
- Subsidy up to ₹78,000 for 2 kW systems; ₹78,000 for 2–3 kW systems.
- Apply at: pmsuryaghar.gov.in
- Eligibility: any household with a sanctioned load of 1 kW and above.

### PM-KUSUM (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan)
- Solar pumps for farmers — 60% subsidy from central + state government.
- Converts diesel irrigation pumps to solar, saving ₹30,000–₹70,000/year in fuel costs.
- Component B: solarisation of existing grid-connected agricultural pumps.
- Contact your state DISCOM (electricity distribution company) to apply.

### Rooftop Solar Programme Phase II
- Net metering allows excess solar power to be exported to the grid for credits.
- Additional 40% central financial assistance for systems up to 3 kW.
- State-level subsidies vary — check your state's renewable energy department.

## India — Electric Vehicle Schemes

### FAME India Phase II (Faster Adoption and Manufacturing of EVs)
- Subsidy up to ₹15,000/kWh on EV batteries (capped at 40% of vehicle cost).
- Covers: electric two-wheelers, three-wheelers, buses, and four-wheelers.
- Extended to March 2024; successor scheme PM E-Drive launched 2024.

### PM E-Drive Scheme (2024–2026)
- ₹10,900 crore allocated to accelerate EV adoption.
- Demand incentives for e-buses, e-trucks, e-ambulances, and e-two/three-wheelers.
- 22,100 fast chargers to be installed at public locations.

## United States — IRA Clean Energy Tax Credits

### Residential Clean Energy Credit (Section 25D)
- 30% tax credit on cost of solar panels, battery storage, wind turbines, geothermal heat pumps.
- No cap on credit amount; applies through 2032.
- Reduces to 26% in 2033, 22% in 2034, then expires.

### Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit (Section 25C)
- Up to $3,200/year in credits for: insulation, heat pumps, windows, doors, electrical upgrades.
- Heat pumps: up to $2,000 credit.
- Insulation and air sealing: up to $1,200 credit.

### Clean Vehicle Tax Credit (Section 30D)
- Up to $7,500 for new electric vehicles meeting battery and assembly requirements.
- Up to $4,000 for used EVs (Section 25E).
- Income limits apply: $150,000 single / $300,000 joint filer.

## United Kingdom — Home Energy Schemes

### ECO4 (Energy Company Obligation, 2022–2026)
- Free or heavily subsidised insulation and low-carbon heating for eligible households.
- Eligibility: receiving means-tested benefits, or living in an energy-inefficient home (EPC D–G) with a low income.
- Covers: loft insulation, cavity wall insulation, solid wall insulation, heat pumps, solar panels.
- Apply via your energy supplier or through a registered ECO4 installer.

### Boiler Upgrade Scheme (BUS)
- £7,500 grant towards an air source heat pump.
- £7,500 grant towards a ground source heat pump.
- £5,000 grant towards a biomass boiler (rural areas only).
- Apply through an MCS-certified installer.

### Great British Insulation Scheme
- Free insulation for homes with EPC rating D–G and council tax band A–D.
- Also available to those on ECO4 eligibility criteria.
