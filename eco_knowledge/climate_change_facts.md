# Climate Change: Key Facts and Individual Actions

## The Science
- Global average temperature has risen by 1.1°C above pre-industrial levels (IPCC AR6, 2021).
- The last decade (2011–2020) was the warmest on record in the past 125,000 years.
- CO₂ concentration in the atmosphere reached 421 ppm in 2023 — the highest in 3 million years.
- Human activities emit approximately 54 billion tonnes of CO₂e per year.
- To limit warming to 1.5°C, global emissions must halve by 2030 and reach net zero by 2050.

## Where Emissions Come From
- Energy (electricity and heat): 34%
- Transport: 16%
- Industry: 24%
- Agriculture, Forestry, Land Use: 22%
- Buildings: 6%
- Waste: 3%

## Individual Carbon Footprint
- Average UK carbon footprint: 10 tonnes CO₂e per person per year.
- Average US carbon footprint: 16 tonnes CO₂e per person per year.
- Average Indian carbon footprint: 2 tonnes CO₂e per person per year.
- Global average: 7 tonnes CO₂e per person per year.
- Target to limit to 1.5°C: 2–2.5 tonnes CO₂e per person by 2030.

## Highest-Impact Individual Actions (Tonnes CO₂e saved per year)
1. **Live car-free**: 2.4 tonnes
2. **Avoid one transatlantic flight**: 1.7 tonnes
3. **Switch to plant-based diet**: 0.5–1.5 tonnes
4. **Buy renewable electricity**: 1.5 tonnes
5. **Install heat pump instead of gas boiler**: 0.9 tonnes
6. **Have one fewer child**: listed in literature but ethically complex
7. **Hang dry laundry**: 0.2 tonnes
8. **Upgrade to LED lighting**: 0.1 tonnes

## Climate Tipping Points
- Arctic sea ice loss: already showing irreversible decline
- Amazon rainforest dieback: risk increases above 1.5°C
- West Antarctic ice sheet collapse: could raise sea levels by 3–5 m
- Permafrost thaw: releases stored methane — a feedback loop
- Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (AMOC) weakening: could disrupt European climate

## Positive Trends
- Renewable energy capacity grew by a record 295 GW in 2022 — solar and wind now cheapest electricity sources in history.
- EV sales reached 14% of global car sales in 2023.
- Over 140 countries have now adopted net-zero targets.
- The cost of solar panels has fallen 90% in the past decade.
- Reforestation projects have planted over 1 billion trees in the past 5 years.

## What Businesses and Governments Must Do
- Phase out fossil fuel subsidies ($7 trillion per year globally, IMF 2023).
- Put a meaningful price on carbon through carbon taxes or cap-and-trade schemes.
- Retrofit all buildings to net-zero standard by 2050.
- Shift agricultural subsidies from meat production to plant-based and regenerative farming.
- Fund climate adaptation in developing nations: $100 billion/year commitment (Paris Agreement).
